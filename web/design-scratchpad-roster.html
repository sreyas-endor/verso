Roster turn states · five directions

Each column is one way for the players bar to say who already drew, who is drawing, and who is next. Every mockup uses the real tokens, the real avatar hues and the real pen glyph, so what reads well here reads the same in the app.

Both states are shown per option, because they are different problems. Mid-turn has a live artist. Handoff does not — the server clears the artist for the intermission and names the next one instead.

The gap this fixes. Today the roster says nothing during a handoff: the artist highlight disappears with artistId, the incoming artist gets no subtitle at all (their index equals turnIndex, which the code reads as “already drew”), and the player after them is wrongly labelled “up next”. Fern is offline in every mockup below, and Gus is eliminated — they are there to show what a new treatment has to avoid colliding with.

Reading these. Option 2 is the boldest at a glance but spends opacity, which is already spoken for twice — Fern at 62% for offline and Gus greyscaled for eliminated. Options 1, 4 and 5 add an affirmative mark instead, so nothing has to be dimmer than anything else. Option 3 wants the same rail the sliding pen already occupies.
