What this implies underneath

  • One field on the wire. MatchSettings.pen_rule, an enum of FREE / ONE_LINE / MAX_FIVE, clamped server-side like every other setting. No numeric limit field, so nothing new to validate a range on.
  • The server already has the enforcement point. internal/room/strokes.go drops a StrokeBegin past MaxStrokesPerTurn; the rule just makes that ceiling 1 or 5 instead of 128. The client-side gauge is a courtesy — authority stays in the room.
  • The lift never ends the turn. Per your call: pen locks, clock runs on. pointercancel fires on app switch, orientation change and palm rejection, and none of those should cost somebody their turn.
  • The budget resets where the point budget already resets — beginTurn() on the server, setDrawingEnabled(true) on the client. One place each, no new lifecycle.
  • Open question in the mock: under One line, should the colour and nib rack go inert the moment the pen touches down? The width and ink are frozen for the stroke either way, so leaving them live is a control that lies.
