Verso — final reveal, per-round canvases

Once a round deals a new word pair and wipes the canvas, the reveal screen stops describing one match and starts describing N independent rounds sharing one imposter. The pair card and the per-player word list have a settled answer — a per-round breakdown. The canvas does not, because MatchEnded carries no strokes: the reveal shows whatever the client's engine still holds, and if each round wipes it, that is the last round only.

Today the canvas is never cleared between rounds (beginRound at internal/room/phase.go:113 leaves r.strokes alone; engine.reset() fires only on LOBBY/ASSIGNING at web/src/main.ts:140), so the reveal shows every mark of the whole match on one sheet. Wiping per round takes that away. These four options are what you can get back.

Open: the canvas

Four ways to spend the reveal's canvas slot. All four are client-only — the engine already keeps a stroke log, so retaining a finished round costs a copy, not a protocol change.

1Final round onlynearest today

Wipe and forget. The reveal shows the canvas that was live when the match ended, exactly as the screen works now.

+ Zero work; nothing to build or lay out. − The rounds you argued hardest about are gone. With 4 rounds you discard three canvases to show one, and Save canvas as PNG silently saves a quarter of the match.

2Round tabs

One full-size stage, a pill per round above it. The pair for the selected round rides along in the card above.

+ Every canvas at full size, no new screen real estate. − Nothing is visible at a glance — you cannot compare round 1 against round 3 without clicking, which is the one comparison a post-mortem actually wants.

3Tiled

Every round's canvas side by side, each captioned with its pair. Nothing to click.

+ The whole match on one screen; comparison is free. − Scales badly. Try 4 above — in the middle column of a 3-column layout each tile is roughly a phone thumbnail, and fine pen work at nib width 3 stops being legible.

4Filmstripsuggested

One full-size stage plus a thumbnail row beneath. The final round leads; click a thumbnail to promote it.

+ Both jobs at once — one canvas readable, all of them present, so you can see at a glance that the match had four drawings and jump to any. + Degrades by scrolling, not by shrinking, so 4 rounds costs nothing. − Most work of the four, and the PNG export needs a decision: the promoted round, or a contact sheet.
